# Art Direction — AI News, August 2026

## Subject
A one-hundred-second current-news dispatch about a paradox: AI is becoming more accessible while its capital, regulatory, and safety stakes become much larger. The visual challenge is to make five factual updates feel like one argument without turning verified stock photography into a generic slideshow.

## Taste profile
- **Design read:** a late-edition international news magazine crossed with a clean systems-control desk.
- **Visual variance:** high; every beat changes its primary subject, geometry, scale, and photo treatment.
- **Motion intensity:** medium-high in the opening and evidence beats, restrained for the conclusion.
- **Information density:** controlled; one headline fact and one support phrase at a time.
- **Reference strategy:** editorial photography, registration marks, printed proof bars, and technical annotation—not an imitation of an existing video package.

## Palette
- `#071015` — near-black blue; analytical, not cinematic-black.
- `#E8F2EE` — paper-white; humane contrast and legible evidence.
- `#30E6C8` — access signal; models moving outward to more people and devices.
- `#FFB84D` — stakes signal; money, regulation, and warning states.
- `#FF5D57` — reserve warning; appears only when control fails.

The turquoise/amber contradiction carries the thesis. It does not become a two-color wash over every frame; each scene has a different emphasis.

## Type personality
- **Heading:** Arial Black / Arial Narrow fallback, uppercase and tightly tracked—the blunt voice of a breaking-news slug.
- **Body:** Arial / Helvetica, sentence case—the neutral voice of evidence.
- **Evidence accent:** Courier New / monospace, tabular numbers and source labels—the voice of an annotated proof sheet.

No remote fonts are required, keeping the render deterministic and offline-safe.

## Motion character
Information arrives like a physical edit desk: firm vertical wipes, clipped mask reveals, registration ticks, and slow photographic drift. Major headlines settle with a damped spring (`damping 18–24`, `stiffness 90–130`); numbers use decisive linear count-ups; transitions use 8–12-frame ease-out windows. No floating glass cards, particle wallpaper, elastic UI bounce, or perpetual zoom.

## Layout & rhythm
A 96-pixel outer safe area and an eight-column invisible grid keep the package disciplined. Photo scale alternates wide / split ledger / edge-device migration / code close-up / server iris / capital stack / official vertical / incident route / typographic closure. Text generally occupies one strong quadrant, leaving negative space instead of centering every beat. Scene cuts follow the approved 9/14/12/9/5/16/13/15/7-second structure.

## Signature device
The **bifurcating signal trace** appears only in scenes 1 and 9. One turquoise line (access) and one amber line (stakes) begin together, split in the hook, then return in the conclusion without fully rejoining. It is a literal, scarce diagram of the editorial thesis—not scaffolding for the middle seven scenes.

## Scene-distinctness inventory
1. **Hook — split signal over the city.** Primary subject: an aerial urban field bisected by the signal trace. First frame: dark city with a narrow scanning slit. Unique because it is the only opening led by the signature device.
2. **OpenAI access — device ledger.** Primary subject: a laptop/phone still cropped into an offset editorial window beside the giant `5.6`. Unique because the photo and numeric ledger share equal weight.
3. **Muse Glimmer — edge migration.** Primary subject: the laptop-and-phone image behind a cloud marker that recedes as the on-device route takes over. Unique because spatial movement from cloud to device carries the claim.
4. **Muse Spark — unlocked weights.** Primary subject: code on a laptop with four learned-parameter blocks rising into view. Unique because model weights become tangible editorial evidence.
5. **Compute bridge — server iris.** Primary subject: a server technician revealed through a rapidly widening vertical iris. Unique because it is the shortest, most physical transition in the film.
6. **Infrastructure — capital stack.** Primary subject: the financial-district photograph behind six accumulating institution bands and the `$500B+` scale. Unique because stacked geometry carries the capital argument.
7. **EU rules — compliance stamps.** Primary subject: a vertical EU-flag photograph stamped `DISCLOSE / LABEL / MACHINE-MARK`. Unique because it reads as an official proof sheet.
8. **Agent risk — route failure.** Primary subject: a bespoke booking-route diagram that changes from green to red over the gym-app photograph. Unique because narrative cause-and-effect is visualized as a path.
9. **Conclusion — three consequence pillars.** Primary subject: `CAPITAL / RULES / CONSEQUENCES` locking under the thesis as the split signal returns. Unique because photography drops away and motion decelerates.

**Distinctness check:** no two scenes share the same primary visual subject. The city and laptop/phone photographs are reused only when their semantic role changes: thesis to capital scale, and product access to edge migration.

## Anti-references
- Not dark-fintech cards, purple AI gradients, glowing brains, holographic robots, or anonymous server-room wallpaper.
- Not a nine-slide title deck with the same hero component moved around.
- Not the stock `Explainer`, `CinematicRenderer`, or any existing OpenMontage scene catalog.
- Not a replica of Phantom Reach, Compound Snowball, Alexandria Fire, or any other atelier precedent.
- Not wall-to-wall subtitles duplicating an identical headline; on-screen slugs summarize while accessibility captions transcribe narration in a separate low rail.
